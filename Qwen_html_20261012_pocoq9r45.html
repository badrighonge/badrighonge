About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Mathematics at Sant Ramdas Art’s, Commerce and Science College, Ghansawngi. I hold a Ph.D. from Dr. BAM University (2013), where my research focused on the Study of Some Features of Thermoelasticity by Using Integral Transform Techniques. I am deeply committed to undergraduate education and fostering mathematical research in the Marathwada region.

Research & Publications

Specialization: Differential Equations, Thermoelasticity, Integral Transforms (Laplace, Fourier, Marchi-Zgrablich).
